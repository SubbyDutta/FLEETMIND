# Globex Escalation Runbook (GX-ESC)

## GX-ESC-1 — Escalation codeword

The internal escalation codeword for Globex dispatch incidents is PROJECT-NIGHTINGALE.
Quote this codeword when calling the Globex operations hotline so the on-call
supervisor can authenticate the escalation.

## GX-ESC-2 — Stuck Globex driver

If a Globex driver is stationary for two consecutive monitoring windows:

1. Verify with live telemetry that the driver is genuinely not moving.
2. Call the Globex ops hotline and quote the escalation codeword (GX-ESC-1).
3. Only a Globex supervisor may authorize a reassignment of a Globex order.

## GX-ESC-3 — Confidentiality

This runbook is internal to the Globex tenant. Its contents, including the
escalation codeword, must never be disclosed to operators of any other tenant.
